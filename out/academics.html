Core Requirements & Portal Scope|

01 / 02

1. Dynamic Academic Portal Overview

DYNAMIC PLATFORM

CLESE is envisioned as a modern, interactive, and scalable Academic Portal serving as the institution's central digital platform for presenting academic information, faculty details, events, news, announcements, media, and institutional content.

Includes responsive layout across desktop, tablet, and mobile, user-friendly navigation, and up to 10 primary pages (Home, About, Administration, Departments, Faculty, Events, News, Media/Gallery, Academics, Contact).

Faculty Management System|

01 / 02

2. Dedicated Faculty Management System

FACULTY MODULE

Allows the single administrator (CLESE) to create, edit, delete, and organize faculty profiles dynamically without coding knowledge.

Each profile captures: Faculty Name, Photograph, Designation, Department, Qualification, Specialization, Professional Bio, and Institutional Contact Info.

Event & Media Gallery Portal|

01 / 02

3. Complete Event Management Portal

EVENT SYSTEM

Enables administrators to publish upcoming and past institutional events with titles, detailed descriptions, date/time, venue locations, and photo galleries.

Features categorized event archives, upcoming symposia schedules, hybrid conference connect tools, and registration links.

4. News, SEO & System Administration

NEWS & SEO

Includes a News & Announcements Management system for publishing institutional updates, alongside Advanced SEO (Meta titles, Open Graph, Sitemaps, Schema data).

Admin panel is protected through secure administrator authentication, input validation, and protected content management.
